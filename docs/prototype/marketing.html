Who is Atlas Nexus for?

Brands and agencies running YouTube creator campaigns, creators looking for collaboration opportunities, and organizations or networks managing channels and contracts. Access and workspace views depend on your role.

How do creators join a campaign?

Creators can register, browse open campaigns after signing in, and apply with their channel information and proposed rates. Agency review and eligibility assessment come before confirmed roster participation and video submission.

What can I measure?

Campaign monitoring uses tracked videos to show available YouTube performance against the saved campaign forecast. Commerce evidence can be imported manually. Forecasts are estimates; automatic order attribution and connected paid-ad performance are not currently available.

Can I manage my partner network here?

Yes. Organizations, partner networks, channels, scoped team permissions, and revenue-share contracts support ongoing network operations alongside the creator partnership workflow.

How do I get started?

Create an account as a Brand, Agency, or Creator and you land straight inside the matching demo workspace. Existing users can log in; Super Admin uses the full demo sign-in. YouTube Brand Campaign is currently labeled Beta.
