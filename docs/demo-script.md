# Atlas Nexus demo walkthrough

Target length: about 2 minutes.

1. Open the homepage and describe the three connected areas: campaign fit, Creator Suites and network hierarchy.
2. Enter as **Agency**. Open **Campaign matching → Aruna · Everyday ritual**. Analyze creator fit and point out the estimated score, evidence, recommendation and sample/Gemini label. Use Fetty Bismo Channel and Astry_id as examples, and clarify that sample video evidence is fixture data.
3. Open **Creator Suites → YouTube Brand Deal Reports**. Show public video counters, generate Executive Insight and explain that Gemini summarizes the selected evidence only.
4. Open the YouTube Channel Report. Call out that its date filter means video publication date while public counters remain cumulative.
5. Visit Channel Profiling briefly, then Public YouTube Analytics. Explain that 40 channel names and rounded subscriber/view counters come from the P2P Labs organization snapshot; video rows and campaign records are demo fixtures, and analytics is independent from campaign state.
6. Switch to **Super Admin** from the profile menu and show the organization → network → channel hierarchy.

When no Gemini key is configured, the actions show sample preview labels. Do not describe these sample outputs as live Gemini responses or fixtures as fetched YouTube data.
